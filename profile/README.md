## What Is Audacity

Audacity is an audio editor and recorder for desktops. Searches such as Audacity audio editor, Audacity multitrack recording, Audacity noise reduction, and Audacity plugins usually mean the same goal: record and finish audio without a paid studio suite.

Audacity keeps the process simple. Record a track, trim the waveform, and apply effects in one window. The Audacity audio editor approach favors readable panels, predictable menus, and a steady edit-listen loop for podcasters, students, musicians, and agencies.

In longer sessions, Audacity connects recording and mixing. Users move from capture to multitrack, then to a finished export without switching tools. That matters when a project grows past a test and needs real edits, fades, and levels.

Audacity also fits common expectations. People checking Audacity multitrack recording or Audacity noise reduction usually want stable playback, clear meters, and a calm path from first launch to a publishable result on everyday hardware.
